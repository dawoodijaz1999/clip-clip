Privacy Policy: clip-clip

Last updated: November 27, 2026

clip-clip is a personal tool used only by its owner. It connects to the owner's own TikTok and Facebook accounts to upload video clips the owner has made.

It stores only the login tokens needed for those uploads, as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository. It does not collect, sell or share data about anyone else.

Access can be removed at any time from the TikTok or Facebook app settings.
